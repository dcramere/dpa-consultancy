Our Story

Founded in Suriname.
Built for the region.

“ A plan is only the beginning. We stay with our clients from the first strategy session to execution and beyond. Servizio Senza Fine

DPA Consultancy was founded in Paramaribo in 2024 with a clear conviction: businesses in the Caribbean and South America deserve advisory work that meets international standards while understanding local realities. Within our first year we grew beyond Suriname into Guyana, French Guiana, Brazil, the CARICOM member islands and states, and the United States. Clients there wanted a partner who understood both their home market and the corridors they were trying to reach.

Our name sets out our method: Development, Planning & Action. Our ethos is Servizio Senza Fine, “service without an end.” Many consultancies hand over a report and move on. We stay. We work alongside leadership teams through implementation, measure what changes, and adjust as markets move. We do not treat an engagement as finished when the plan is delivered. For us, that is where the partnership starts.
